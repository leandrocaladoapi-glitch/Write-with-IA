## Diagnosing generic AI prose

First, the honest framing: "AI prose" is not a *look*; it's a **cluster of habits** that happen to co-occur in model-default output because they're high-probability completions (Chapter 5's mechanism: nothing about a generated paragraph *proves* a machine wrote it — *your* unanchored sentences can carry the same cluster, and machine sentences under tight locks sometimes don't). The diagnostic below therefore says "generic-register symptoms," not "AI tells" — you're auditing *the habits*, wherever they came from, including your own tired Tuesdays. (Inverse honesty: hand-written prose fails this checklist all the time; the checklist is *style-neutral about tools* and *specific about patterns* — which is what makes it usable in the Ch 12 line pass, where provenance is irrelevant and the sentence either works or wears the symptoms.)

The four symptom families, each with a **test you can run on a page**:

1. **Hedging inflation.** *Test:* highlight every qualifier — "seemed," "somewhat," "in a way," "a kind of," "almost as if," "perhaps." Then *delete every one* and read again. If the passage lost precision, some hedges were load-bearing (real uncertainty earns its word); if the passage got *sharper* — the hedges were **confidence-laundering**, sentences hedging so they can never be wrong, which is machine-safe-completion's signature move (and yours, when you're writing to avoid critique). Density tell: more than one hedge per three sentences in narrative voice = audit for laundering. The cure isn't *always* assertiveness (some narrators genuinely wobble — a *chosen* wobble is style; a *diffuse* wobble is default).

2. **Thesaurus weather.** *Test:* read only the adjectives, adverbs, and figurative phrases aloud, skipping the nouns and verbs. Generic register *shows* instantly in this stripped channel: the synonyms arrive *wrong-register* for your book (your plainspoken fishing-town story suddenly has "ethereal" light and "cacophonous" gulls — the thesaurus reached for *fancier* because fancier *reads* as effortful), or *safe-familiar* ("old," "faint," "gentle" ×6 per page). Two signatures: **upgrade-itis** (words dressed above the narrator's register — diction *disconnected from voice* — the sharpest single detector, because *your* voice has a known class-level and machine-default operates a class above or below it) and **repeat-drift** (the same "nice" intensifier installed three pages running — "deeply" doing quiet unpaid labor — a pattern *within* one author's range is tic (style-sheet territory), *outside* it is drift).

3. **Rhythmic monotony.** *Test:* mark sentence lengths as a column of numbers down the margin of one paragraph-heavy page. Generic register clusters: sentences stacking in a narrow band (the 14–22-word corridor — model-default's comfort zone), paragraph after paragraph — *because cadence averages* (Chapter 5: generation regresses toward typical, and typical prose is *evenly medium*). The failure isn't medium sentences (you need them — they're the tram tracks); it's **the absence of the irregular**: a paragraph where *nothing* is 6 words, nothing is 34, no sentence breaks the paragraph's own emerging pattern *on purpose*. Human voices are *uneven in characteristic ways* — your Chapter 1 baseline's rhythm, for all its craft-problems, was *yours* (mark its length-column too: you'll see *your* signature imbalance — that's what you're trying to keep while smoothing the rest).

4. **Empty lyricism / reflection-tax.** *Test:* circle every sentence whose job is *commenting on the scene's feeling* rather than advancing or sensing it ("and in that moment, she understood what loss truly meant"). Then ask, per circle: did the *scene* already carry this — was the understanding *performed* two lines earlier by an action? Generic register **pays a reflection-tax**: a closing meditation appended to emotional beats *by default*, because emotional completions pattern-match to *moral-summary* completions in its diet (the trailing-summary ending from Ch 4's double-ending list, promoted here to a whole-family symptom). Related sub-signature: **symmetrical balance** — clauses arriving in matched pairs ("not just X, but Y" — and again — and again): balance by *default* rather than by *meaning* is oratorical emptiness wearing craft's clothes; real balance earns itself once per page at most, at the moments that deserve ceremony.

**The four-minute diagnostic (the checklist, runnable on any two pages):**

```text
GENERIC-REGISTER DIAGNOSTIC (2 pages, cold read)
[ ] Hedges: count > 6 → strip-all-and-retest (precision lost = keep
    some; sharper = laundering found)
[ ] Diction class: underline 10 modifiers aloud — any arriving from
    a class your narrator wouldn't use? (upgrade-itis / safe-familiar)
[ ] Rhythm column: sentence-length numbers — narrow-band stretch of
    3+ paragraphs flagged; missing extremes (any 6-word or 30+
    sentence in view?)
[ ] Reflection-tax: circled commentaries vs. performed beats — any
    appended after the scene already showed it? any symmetrical
    "not-X-but-Y" pairs beyond one per page?
[ ] Swear-level honesty: could *anyone's* narrator have written
    these pages? (the existential test — if yes, the other four
    found why)
→ Output: symptom counts + the WORST offender sentence quoted
  (the quote feeds PC-26's context — diagnosis produces the
  surgery's first target, not a vague malaise)
```

Run it on the *current-draft sample* from the hook now, before reading on — counts in your margin. (If the page came back clean: either your anchors have held beautifully — good; go run it on your *machine-heaviest chapter* anyway, the one the mode-log's `co-draft` rows name — diagnosis belongs where generation density lives.) Two calibration notes before tools: the diagnostic is *quantitative on purpose* (counts and columns beat "feels generic" — the felt version is unfalsifiable and demoralizing; six hedges is a *finding*, an atmosphere is a mood), and **severity scales with your register's tightness** (a deliberately ornate literary voice gets *more* lyricism budget than your baseline-minimalist — the style sheet, coming below, is what makes the diagnostic's thresholds *yours* rather than universal — the checklist without the sheet is other people's taste).

## The de-AI pass

Diagnosis in hand, surgery: **PC-26** — the pass that keeps structure and replaces *voice*, the inverse of Chapter 10's anchors (there: your voice established *before* generation; here: your voice re-established *after*, at the sentence level):

> PROMPT CARD PC-26 — De-AI prose pass
>
> ```text
> <!-- PC-26 v1 · tested 2026-09 · generic, no model-specific syntax -->
> Role: sentence surgeon executing MY revoicing — you remove
> symptoms and preserve skeleton; you do not add content, images,
> or opinions; when unsure whether a line is load-bearing, LEAVE
> IT (flag instead).
> Context: my STYLE SHEET (banned constructions + my rhythm notes +
> diction-class statement + tics-to-keep — pasted) + my diagnosis
> output (worst-offender quote + symptom counts) + the passage.
> Targets (from the four families — apply only where flagged):
> H1 hedge-strip (keep load-bearing uncertainty — list kept ones)
> H2 diction-demote (upgrade-itis → my narrator's class; safe-
>    familiar → my class at *specificity*, not fanciness)
> H3 rhythm-break (mark narrow-band stretches; propose ONE
>    split/merge/fragment per stretch — minimum intervention to
>    restore irregularity, not maximal)
> H4 reflection-tax cut (delete commentaries the scene performed;
>    break symmetry pairs at ONE side per pair — which side I decide
>    from my sheet)
> Preserve absolutely: plot facts, proper nouns, locked POV/tense/
> distance, [!mine] markers, any line I've style-sheeted as a KEEP.
> Output: (a) table: ORIGINAL → EDITED + symptom-tag + keep-flags;
> (b) clean passage; (c) counts: hedges −n, class-changes n,
> rhythm-breaks n, reflections cut n. I adjudicate rows as in PC-21;
> rejections final.
> ```

Read the surgery's *ethics of editing*: **leave-if-unsure + flag** (the conservative bias that keeps this pass from deleting *your* deliberate oddities the machine can't recognize as deliberate — the `[!mine]` and KEEP-list do the recognizing *for* it; unknown-odd lines get *flagged to you*, which is the pass's core asymmetry: it's allowed to *find*, barely allowed to *change* — adjudication-heavy by design, like every pass whose mistakes are invisible in reverse); **symmetry broken at ONE side, you choose** (the balance-symptom's fix isn't *anti-balance*, it's *your* balance — which side of the pair survives is a taste decision the sheet encodes and the machine *cannot* infer — golden rule at clause scale); **demote-to-your-class, not to blandness** (H2's careful wording: the fix for upgrade-itis is *register-matching* — "palpable silence" → "a silence with *weight* you could feel in the room" if weight-at-that-class is your narrator's idiom, NOT → "a quiet silence" — the pass *serves the sheet*, and a pass run *without* a pasted sheet degenerates into a house-register *launderer* (the failure mode to name once: de-AI without a style sheet just installs *different* generic prose — the sheet is the instrument; the card is only its executor); and **counts at the end** (the diagnostic's numbers re-run post-surgery — the pass's completion signal is *symptom counts trending toward your sheet's thresholds*, not a vibe of improvement — convergence measurable, circle impossible: two runs with <10% delta = the passage is done *for this pass*, next passage, next symptom family — bounded, like Ch 12 demands).

What the pass deliberately does *not* touch: **structure, beats, and adjudicated machine contributions** — the scene's architecture (the skeleton, the composite from Ch 10's three-ways) was *yours via decisions rows*; de-AI is *re-voicing*, not re-deciding. The flow to internalize (the full pipeline the last five chapters built): *anchor before → locks during → mark at drafting → blind-test voices → diagnose generically → de-AI surgically → style-sheet the survivors* — each layer cheaper than the last, each gate owned by you, and by the end of the chain the machine's fingerprints on the page are **either gone or adopted** — nothing left *ambiguous*, which is the actual definition of voice-owned prose this book has been converging on since Chapter 1's taste lecture.

## Imitation → variation → voice

Diagnosis and surgery maintain the voice you have; the ladder below *builds* the one you're still becoming — the oldest program in creative education, updated for a tool that makes each rung cheaper:

**Rung 1 — Imitation (copy with intent).** Select a living-or-dead writer whose *specific* features you covet — not "good prose" (unselectable) but "how she handles clause-length under pressure," "how *he* uses monosyllables after polysyllables" — and **copy a page by hand, then from memory.** The hand-copy teaches *decisions you can't see from reading alone* (every sentence you retype *is* a decision you've now physically agreed with); the memory-copy immediately reveals what you *didn't* internalize (you'll paraphrase — and your paraphrase's *drift* is diagnostic: the places you "misremembered" are where your habits resisted theirs — gold for the ladder). AI's role on this rung: **PC-27's analysis half** — paste the original page and ask for a *feature inventory* ("list the techniques this page uses: sentence-length pattern (numbers), image sources, hedge frequency, balance usage, paragraph architecture — features only, no quality judgments") — the inventory is a *checklist of what to copy*, making the hand-copy targeted (you're now practicing *features*, not vibes); the *copying itself stays in your hands* (the whole neurological argument of the rung would evaporate if the machine did it — analysis may be delegated; **acquisition may not**, it's rep-based skill like the taste-gate reps of Ch 1, just aimed at syntax).

**Rung 2 — Variation (the controlled mutation).** Take *your* imitation-page (now semi-installed) and produce **three variations that break exactly one axis each**: (a) same techniques, *your* subject matter (their clause-architecture describing *your* fishing town — the transfer test: do the moves survive without their content?); (b) same content, *opposite* their tendencies (their balanced clauses → all your ragged ones; their long meditations → cut every reflection-tax sentence — running the diagnostic *on their style* and fixing it reveals *which of their habits you actually wanted* vs. which you were admiring on authority); (c) same features at *half-length* (compression exposes the load-bearing 40% — if the imitation needs every ornament to work, you loved the ornament, not the method). PC-27's generation half *proposes* variation (b) and (c) drafts *labeled as exercises* — machine text permitted here *explicitly* because this rung's product is *muscle memory of contrast*, not pages: you read the machine's mutated versions *against yours* and articulate the difference ("its half-length cut my favorite image — because the image was *decoration in the variation*, load-bearing in mine" — that sentence is voice-knowledge, worth more than either draft). The rung's completion test: write 300 fresh words on *your* material using *one chosen axis* of the inventory — read them beside your baseline: **different from your old self, recognizable as no one else** — imitation that *moved* you without *replacing* you = one rung climbed.

**Rung 3 — Voice (the deliberate signature).** Voice, defined operationally for this book: **the set of stylistic choices you make *consistently under pressure*** — not your vocabulary on a good day (that's mood) but your habits when tired, when drafting fast, when the machine's suggestions look *good enough* (the pressure is the definers: anyone can be stylish at leisure; voice is what you do at 11 p.m. with a deadline). The rung's work is therefore *cataloging* rather than inventing: from your style sheet (below), your runs-2 deviations, and your baseline-vs-current gaps, you extract **three to five named features that are *yours* and that you'll defend** — maybe: *clipped fragments after questions* (your rhythm tell), *diction pitched one class below the scene's emotion* (your register choice — feeling delivered in plain words because ornament would lie), *reflections performed only through objects* (your answer to the reflection-tax — never appended, always enacted: the soup pot *is* the grief). Naming them does three jobs: they become **locks you protect** (the sheet's KEEP list — PC-26 preserves them by name, PC-14's anchors *are* them, PC-18's fingerprint-logic runs on them — your features are your characters' anti-flattening cousin: *author-level* fingerprints); they become **the diagnostic's baseline** (symptoms are *deviations from your named features* — the checklist stops being other-people's taste); and they become **checkable claims about originality** — which is where this chapter's last理论 lands.

**Originality as recombination + lived specificity.** The anxious age's question — *"is originality even possible when the machine remixes everything?"* — dissolves under this book's definitions twice over. *Recombination:* every writer you admire is already a recombination (their influences *documented*, their originality lying in *which* combinations, under *what* pressures, executed with *what consistency* — your voice-features × your obsessions from Ch 8's why-me file × your constraint bank's material = a combination whose *specific instance* no training set contains as a *choice*, because choices-with-names-under-pressure are exactly what the distribution doesn't have (it has their *outputs*, not their *defended commitments*). And *lived specificity* — the second engine — is structurally immune: the machine completes *representations* of experiences; it does not have *yours* (the Tuesday your father's depot closed and the particular smell of its parts crib — Chapter 1's "true details" and Chapter 4's "owned concrete" at maximum stakes: prose that could only exist because *you* were there is original the way a fingerprint is original — not novel-with-a-capital-N (the machine's problem, Ch 1's inventory) but *specific*, which was always the operable kind). The ladder's honest claim, then: **style can be practiced into consistency (rungs 1–2), originality can only be *kept specific* (rung 3 + the why-me file + the sheets)** — the machine touches the first and *demonstrates the absence* of the second, which is why a voice-owning writer in 2026 has *less* competition at the level that matters, not more: most generated text never gets specific enough to collide with anyone's particular life — your whole advantage is doing *that*, on purpose, with a sheet.

## Your style sheet

The chapter's central artifact (template — copy to `bible/style-sheet.md`, v1 today, revised at every line pass):

```text
STYLE SHEET — [project or personal]            v1 · 2026-09-XX

1. DICTION CLASS (one line — narrator's register, stated as a
   range): "plain, physical words; abstract nouns only when they're
   also weather/measurements; one Latinate flourish per chapter,
   chosen."
2. RHYTHM NOTE (your signature imbalance — from baseline's
   length-column): "medium default, but every 4th paragraph needs
   a runt (<7w) and periodic sentences are mine — max one 'lovely'
   30-w per page."
3. BANNED CONSTRUCTIONS (growing list — machine-default +
   self-tics caught by diagnostics; each entry dated):
   - hedge-strings >1 per 3 sentences (2026-09)
   - "palpable/ethereal/unwavering" class (upgrade-itis exemplars)
   - emotion-as-weather ("guilt rose like tide") (from PC-14 v2)
   - "not just X, but Y" beyond one per page
   - reflection-tax closers after performed beats
   - "she couldn't help but" (your own Ch-1 tic — baseline-
     harvested: tics-to-kill from the *unaided* record)
4. KEEPS (deliberate oddities PC-26 must preserve — [!mine] list):
   - fragments after questions (feature 1)
   - emotion-in-plain-words (feature 2)
   - objects-performed-feelings only (feature 3)
   - typos of voice? NO — dialect spelled as spoken only in
     [X]'s speech, noted per-character in bible §2
5. VERB POLICY: active by default; three passive slots allowed
   per chapter where agency *should* blur (reporting, dread).
6. FIGURE BUDGET: one simile per ~1,000w; sources restricted to
   [your domains: weather, mechanics, tide]; no metaphor for
   emotions directly — objects only (ties to feature 3).
7. REVISION-DATE + NEXT-REVIEW (line-pass trigger — sheets live
   at pass 4, v-bump when a ban graduates from a diagnostic).
```

Sheet-writing craft notes: **items 3 and 4 are the engine** (bans *from evidence* — every entry cites the diagnostic or baseline-tic that caught it, so the sheet's authority is *your own record*, immune to "whose taste is this?" — and keeps *equally* evidenced, or the sheet becomes a purity-spiral that sanded your weirdness off); **item 1's range-wording** (a single-word class declaration like "plain" gets violated by every tool reading it — ranges with *inclusions and one budgeted exception* ("one Latinate flourish per chapter") are what machine-passes can actually *execute against* — the sheet is a contract *with instruments*, which is why its format looks half-essay, half-spec); **the growth path** (v1: from baseline-tics + this chapter's diagnostics, ~15 lines; v2 at your first line pass: real hits added; v3: PC-26's keep-flags revealing *features you didn't know you had* — the sheet's most valuable entries are the ones *discovered in your own defended revisions* — decisions-file mining for style rows: every "kept my wording over the machine's, because ___" is a feature *caught in the act*; the sheet is that column of your ledger, systematized). Maintenance cadence: **read at line-pass entry, bump at line-pass exit, full re-read quarterly** (the bans that *stopped firing* graduate to a "cold" subsection — a ban you haven't triggered in three passes was either internalized (feature now automatic — move it to KEEP-side pride) or *wrong* for the register (delete with a note — the sheet may *correct* itself, versioned like everything else this book has touched: a style sheet that never revises itself is the static-outline disease wearing academic costume).

## Worked example: de-AI a paragraph, move by move

Real surgery — a co-drafted paragraph from Example A's storm-night stretch (machine-heaviest region per its mode-log), diagnosis first, then PC-26's table, then the adjudication:

**Before (diagnostic run — counts in margin):** *"It was, in a way, a kind of quiet that seemed to settle over the house as the power failed — a palpable silence, not just empty but somehow waiting, as if the building itself were holding its breath in anticipation of what might happen next. Mara felt a sense of something like recognition at this moment, and she couldn't help but think that the darkness had always been there, latent within these rooms, patient and unspoken."*

**Diagnostic counts:** hedges/fillers: *"in a way," "a kind of," "seemed," "somehow," "as if," "something like"* = 6 (threshold blown); upgrade-itis: *"palpable," "latent," "unspoken"* (class: MFA-brochure — narrator's class is plain-physical); rhythm: 34w / 26w / 28w — narrow-band corridor, zero runts; reflection-tax: sentence 2's "recognition" + sentence 3's entire meditation appended *after* the performed beat (power failed = the scene *showed* waiting — the building-holding-its-breath line *comments* on it symmetrically — "not just empty but somehow waiting" is the X-but-Y pair, doubled with "patient and unspoken"); worst-offender quote (feeds PC-26's context): *"a palpable silence, not just empty but somehow waiting."*

**PC-26's output (table abridged; sheet pasted: banned §3 entries all cited):**

```text
| ORIGINAL | EDITED | tag |
```

**After (clean passage):** *"Quiet settled when the power failed. A silence with the house's weight in it — holding, like a held breath. Mara stopped at the breaker. Darkness had been in these rooms all along. Patient."* — and **the adjudication** (rows you'd write): *"KEEP the cut's core per flag (journal-payoff sign) — machine correctly identified load-bearing and left the skeleton; 'held breath' image: MY KEEP-side (sheet §4 objects-performed-feelings — the house breathes = object-feeling ✓); 'Quiet settled' runt: rhythm-break accepted — my rhythm note says every 4th paragraph needs a runt; one REJECT: its 'like a held breath' kept my doubled 'holding/held' — the repetition is [!mine] (marking habit paid: the preserve-rule caught its own near-miss)."*

*Commentary — the five moves that matter:* the paragraph went from 62 words of generic weather to 24 of *house-class plain physics* **with the architecture unchanged** (the performed beat still lands where the draft's decisions put it — de-AI ≠ re-outline); every cut *cited its sheet entry* (the sheet-as-contract, executing — and note the pass got *better* as the sheet got real: PC-26 without §3's specific bans would have produced "different but still someone else's" prose); the **leave-flag saved the meditation's core** (the conservative bias *working* — the machine couldn't know "darkness-always-there" was structural, so it *asked* instead of killing — and your adjudication, citing *ch10's payoff-sign*, answered with the outline open: style-surgery consulting structure, the pipeline's layers *talking*); the **[!mine] near-miss** (its cleaned version *smoothed your deliberate holding-held echo* — caught because the marker was in the paste — the preserve-list's value demonstrated in the pass's own first run: unmarked, your echo would have died politely, and *no diagnostic would have flagged a deletion* — the asymmetry (symptoms find additions, markers protect *intentional* oddities) is why the full pipeline needs *all* its layers: diagnosis misses what it can't see was deliberate, only *you* can mark deliberate); and the count-trend (**6 hedges → 0, corridor broken, tax paid down**) — the pass *converged on the sheet's thresholds* and stopped (bounded — no vibe of "maybe prettier still?" — that itch is F-07's ghost, and the count-arithmetic keeps it exorcised).

Apply the same six-move reading to *your* worst paragraph: diagnose with counts → sheet pasted → flags respected → adjudicate with your outline open → recount. The paragraph-level version of this chapter, repeated as a habit, *is* the voice program: maintenance by instruments, growth by ladder, originality by specificity — three systems, one name on the cover.

## Pitfalls

> PITFALL — **Voice erosion (F-09).** *Symptom:* the draft converges on AI-default *gradually enough to be invisible session-by-session* — quarterly, your pages read like a competent stranger's; beta residue-images (Ch 13's Q8) come back *generic* where you designed specific; the Ch 12 voice-audit's shuffle-test drops below your done-criteria's threshold; you catch yourself *unable to produce a paragraph unaided* that matches your own sheet (the deepest symptom: the voice now lives in the tool, not in you — anchors feel like scaffolding for a building that forgot its foundation). *Why it happens:* **the pull never turns off** (Ch 5's center-of-mass is a permanent force — erosion is the *default direction* of assisted drafting under incomplete layers; each skipped layer (anchor → marking → blind-test → diagnosis → de-AI) *raises the leak-rate* slightly — the compound-interest structure of F-09: no single skipped session ruins anything; thirty skipped sessions *rebrand your narrator*); **house-register seduction under deadline** (the machine's smooth paragraphs are *faster to accept at 11 p.m.* — the acceptance curve and the fatigue curve are the same curve — mode-log cross-refs will show your drift clustering on your tired days, exactly like Ch 11's Sunday-smiles); **sheet-staleness** (a sheet not read at pass entry is decorative — bans you've stopped enforcing *silently stop binding* — the tool reads whatever's in the paste, and stale pastes license drift); and **the identity dodge** (symptom families are easier to spot in *others'* prose than admit in yours — "generic" is a criticism writers fear applying to *their own darlings*, so the diagnostic gets run on chapters you *suspect* rather than the ones mode-logs *name* — comfort-diagnosis, the evasion that keeps the drift column looking empty while chapter 9 is colonized). *Fix (layered — matching the pipeline, in drift-detected order):* (1) **stop the leak *upstream* tonight:** re-verify the anchor discipline's functional test (Ch 10's "would generation lose its hinges without this paragraph?") on your next scene — if anchors were decorative, regenerate the worst affected stretch *with beats + locks* (the Ch 10 fallback machinery — drift is *recoverable at drafting-precision* before it's expensive at de-AI-precision); (2) **measure before mood:** re-run the four-minute diagnostic on the mode-log's *machine-heaviest* chapter and on a *self-suspected* chapter (the comfort-diagnosis antidote is *mandated sampling* — the two columns you can't dodge), symptom counts recorded as the *audit baseline* (Ch 7's monthly voice-check now has numbers, not vibes); (3) **surgery with the full kit:** PC-26 per worst paragraph — sheet pasted, leave-flags honored, `[!mine]` markers *written before the paste* (the near-miss lesson: mark *first*, pass *second*), adjudication rows with your outline open (structure-suspects cited, not guessed), counts re-run to thresholds (bounded exit — two runs, done, next paragraph: the pass version of F-07-prevention applied to voice); (4) **rebuild the *generative* voice, not just the cosmetic one:** if the unaided-paragraph test (Try's deepest probe) now fails *badly* — you can't write sheet-compliant prose without the tool — run the ladder *rung 1 hard* for two weeks (hand-copy + memory-copy daily, one feature at a time): imitation-at-daily-rep is how acquisition repairs *sedimented-out* habits — the model's smoothness un-learned by your hands' unevenness, the oldest technology for this exact problem still the best; (5) **close the loop where it entered:** draft-log the drift's *cause-session* (which mode, which fatigue, which missing layer — one honest row per colonized stretch: "co-draft, day 14 streak-tired, anchors skipped" — the *pattern* of those rows is your personal erosion-rhythm — schedule *hand-first sessions* proactively at those slots next month (drift-prevention as *calendar design*, the deepest form of the fix: you cannot skip the layer on the day you *planned* for the layer because you know yourself). *Prevention (the steady state — the five layers as one habit-stack, each cheaper than the last, total cost ≈ 15 minutes per scene):* anchor functionally → locks stated → mark incrementally → blind-test dialogue-heavy chapters → diagnose one page per drafting week → de-AI only on *counts-over-threshold* → sheet bump at every pass exit → quarterly baseline comparison as the *scheduling mechanism* for the whole program. The stack reads like a lot until you price its alternative: **a finished book whose voice you can no longer produce, defend, or recognize** — the book's whole argument (Ch 1's golden rule at maximum stakes) says that outcome isn't a style problem; it's an *authorship* problem, and authorship, once more: **you decide — every sentence that keeps your name.**

## Key takeaways

- **"Generic AI prose" is a symptom cluster, not provenance:** hedges (confidence-laundering — strip-and-retest), thesaurus weather (class-mismatch is the sharpest detector), rhythmic monotony (the 14–22 corridor — absence of *designed* irregularity), reflection-tax and symmetry-by-default — each with a *countable* test; diagnosis is quantitative, tool-neutral, and yours the moment the sheet exists to set thresholds.
- **The de-AI pass is re-voicing, not re-deciding:** PC-26 runs on your pasted sheet + diagnosis quote, leaves-if-unsure with flags, breaks symmetry at *your* chosen side, preserves `[!mine]` (mark *before* pasting — the near-miss is the rule's proof), counts to thresholds (bounded), and never touches adjudicated structure — without a sheet, it just installs *different* generic prose; the sheet is the instrument, the card is only its executor.
- **Voice is built on the three-rung ladder:** imitation (feature inventories delegated, *copying hand-held* — acquisition never outsourced), variation (one-axis mutations — transfer, inversion, compression — where machine drafts are labeled exercises and your articulations of the difference are the real product), voice (your choices *consistently under pressure* — cataloged as three-to-five defended, named features that become KEEP-locks, diagnostic baselines, and fingerprint logic).
- **Originality survives the machine as recombination *plus lived specificity*:** defended commitments under named pressure aren't in any distribution, and your Tuesdays are structurally uncompletable — specific beats novel-with-a-capitol-N, and *most* generated prose never gets specific enough to collide with yours.
- **Erosion is default-direction and layered-fixed:** anchors → locks → marking → blind-tests → weekly diagnosis → count-bounded surgery → sheet-bumps → quarterly baseline comparisons — each layer cheap, each skip compounding, cause-rows designing the calendar that prevents the skip — because at F-09's endgame, the question was never stylistic: a voice you can no longer produce unaided is a name you can no longer honestly sign. Sheets, ladders, and counts exist so you always can.
