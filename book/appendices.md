## Appendix B — Workbook Keys & Extra Exercises (~2,500 words)

**Purpose:** sample solutions for selected `Try`/`Stretch` exercises + extra drills.

- Keys are **sample responses, not correct answers** — say so up front.
- Extra drills: 10-minute warm-ups organized by chapter.
- The `Project` exercises are not keyed (they build the reader's own story).

| F-08 | Feedback triage paralysis | Ch 13 |
| F-09 | Voice erosion: draft sounds like "AI prose" | Ch 14 |

Each entry follows: **Symptom** (what you see) → **Diagnosis** (why it happens) →
**Fix** (prompt or process change, with example) → **Prevention** (habit or checklist).

## Appendix E — Research Notes & Sources (~1,500 words)

**Purpose:** the printed tip of `research/claim-register.md` — what the book asserts,
how confident we are, and where to check.

- Per-chapter source notes (craft sources, official docs, studies).
- Perishable figures table: claim, value, as-of date, where to re-check.
- Explicit statement of what was **not** verified and why (cut or hedged).

## Appendix F — This Book's AI-Use Log (~1,500 words)

**Purpose:** practice what we preach — a transparent log of AI assistance in producing
this book.

**Columns:** date · chapter/section · tool (generic role) · task (outline/prose/edit/code) ·
human review (who, what changed) · notes.

**Rule (from style guide):** no AI-drafted passage ships without human revision and a
logged review. The log itself is part of the book's argument for transparency norms.
